# 骑士篇背景大图（剧情过场）的来源

工具：codex CLI（`codex exec`）调用内置 image_gen，1536×1024。没有下载外部素材。
参考图：
- `ref_style.png`（现有的 Boss CG，只用来对齐画风）；
- `ref_characters.png`（游戏里的立绘 / 行走图，对齐人物长相和配色）。

导入：`tools/art/import_story_scenes.py`（按每张的焦点裁成 16:9 的 1536×864）→ `assets/generated/story_scenes/<名字>.png`。
游戏里用过场动作 `bg` 摆上去（`StoryBackdrop`）。

## 通用要求（每张都有）

> Use your built-in image generation tool (image_gen) to create the image described below, each saved in the current directory under the given filename (PNG). Do not edit or create any other files. After saving it, reply with just the filename.
> Common format for EVERY image: a high-resolution, highly detailed, polished anime illustration (story event CG for a cute fantasy RPG), landscape canvas 1536x1024. Match the painting style of the attached ref_style.png (soft luminous lighting, delicate line art, rich pastel colors, sparkles and flower petals, glossy eyes) — use it ONLY for the style, not its pose or composition. Character designs MUST follow the attached ref_characters.png: PINK princess = long wavy pink hair, small pink crown with a red heart gem, pink-and-white frilly princess dress; BLUE princess = long pale-blue hair with blue roses, silver-blue tiara, blue-and-white frilly dress, calm cool expression; party HOST = young woman with long flowing orange-red hair and a flower crown, orange-red festival dress; CAPTAIN = tall man with tied-back reddish-brown hair and silver plate armor with a navy cape; GRANNY = small old woman pharmacist with a grey hooded cap and green apron; TOM and LILY = two small village children (Tom a boy with brown hair and a blue shirt, Lily a girl with brown pigtails and a pink dress); ENGINEER = sturdy craftsman with a yellow work cap and a dark work coat. The two princesses are ADULT women drawn with modest, fully covered dresses; the whole image is wholesome and non-sexual (no cleavage focus, no suggestive poses). Setting: a cozy underground fantasy town (warm lanterns, wooden houses, stone streets, a cavern ceiling far above instead of sky unless stated). The important characters and objects stay inside the central 16:9 band (leave the top and bottom ~80px as expendable background). Leave the bottom area visually calm (a dialogue box will cover the bottom quarter). No text, no letters, no numbers, no UI, no watermark, no signature.
