# 骑士篇背景大图（剧情过场）的来源

工具：codex CLI（`codex exec`）调用内置 image_gen，1536×1024。没有下载外部素材。
参考图：
- `ref_style.png`（现有的 Boss CG，只用来对齐画风）；
- `ref_characters.png`（游戏里的立绘 / 行走图，对齐人物长相、配色和服装：粉色 `portraits.png`、蓝色 `blue_princess/魔法吟诵.png`、派对主人 `bonfire_host_portraits/portraits1.PNG`）。
  服装：两位公主和派对主人都是露肩装、前短的裙子（正面能看到一点大腿）；粉色白色中长筒袜，蓝色白色荷叶边短袜。

导入：`tools/art/import_story_scenes.py`（按每张的焦点裁成 16:9 的 1536×864）→ `assets/generated/story_scenes/<名字>.png`。
游戏里用过场动作 `bg` 摆上去（`StoryBackdrop`）。

## 通用要求（每张都有）

> Use your built-in image generation tool (image_gen) to create the image described below, each saved in the current directory under the given filename (PNG). Do not edit or create any other files. After saving it, reply with just the filename.
> Common format for EVERY image: a high-resolution, highly detailed, polished anime illustration (story event CG for a cute fantasy RPG), landscape canvas 1536x1024. Match the painting style of the attached ref_style.png (soft luminous lighting, delicate line art, rich pastel colors, sparkles and flower petals, glossy eyes) — use it ONLY for the style, not its pose or composition. Character designs and OUTFITS MUST match the attached ref_characters.png exactly (same costumes in every scene, drawn in this detailed anime style instead of chibi): PINK princess = long wavy pink hair with red ribbon bows, small gold tiara with a red gem, red heart gem at the chest, OFF-SHOULDER pink-and-white layered frilly dress whose skirt is short in front (hem above the knee, a little of the thighs shows in front) and longer at the back, WHITE KNEE-HIGH SOCKS, pink Mary-Jane shoes with bows; BLUE princess = long pale-blue hair with two small side buns, blue roses and blue ribbons, silver-blue crystal tiara, calm cool expression, OFF-SHOULDER white dress with detached puffy short sleeves and big royal-blue ribbons, skirt hem just above the knee (a little of the thighs shows in front), SHORT white frilly ANKLE socks, blue shoes; party HOST = young woman with very long wavy orange-red hair, a small gold crown with red flowers and ribbons, OFF-SHOULDER orange-red dress of flame-like layered frills, short in front with long flame-shaped trailing layers, red ribbon-laced sandals; CAPTAIN = tall man with tied-back reddish-brown hair and silver plate armor with a navy cape; GRANNY = small old woman pharmacist with a grey hooded cap and green apron; TOM and LILY = two small village children (Tom a boy with brown hair and a blue shirt, Lily a girl with brown pigtails and a pink dress); ENGINEER = sturdy craftsman with a yellow work cap and a dark work coat. The princesses and the host are ADULT women; the outfits are their normal princess dresses, and the whole image stays wholesome and non-sexual (natural poses, no cleavage focus, no suggestive angles). Setting: a cozy underground fantasy town (warm lanterns, wooden houses, stone streets, a cavern ceiling far above instead of sky unless stated). The important characters and objects stay inside the central 16:9 band (leave the top and bottom ~80px as expendable background). Leave the bottom area visually calm (a dialogue box will cover the bottom quarter). No text, no letters, no numbers, no UI, no watermark, no signature.
